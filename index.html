The teacher view

One dashboard for the whole class

The same management system, in your own space, with nobody else's students in it.

Students & enrollment

Create accounts, enrol them on courses, hand over sign-in details, and switch access on or off. Parent contacts kept alongside.

Courses, units & lessons

Build a course out of units and lessons, attach recordings and PDFs, and schedule each unit to open on the morning of its session.

Question bank & tests

Keep every question in one bank, then build a test from it in a few clicks — with a time limit, a pass mark and an open/close window.

Progress & reports

See who has watched what, who has sat which test, and who is falling behind — per student, per course, at a glance.

Announcements

Tell the whole class, or one course, what is happening — pinned to the top of every student's dashboard until it is read.

Assistants, with limits

Bring in help and choose exactly what they may touch — students, content, tests — without handing over your whole space.
