What's inside

Everything for your studying, in one place

Your lessons, your tests and your marks — together, and nothing you have to set up yourself.

Recordings

Every session recorded and kept, unit by unit, so a lesson you missed — or one you want again before a test — is always there.

Materials

Notes, sheets and PDFs attached to the lesson they belong to, instead of scattered through a chat you have to scroll back through.

Tests, marked instantly

Sit a test with the time on screen and get your score the moment you hand it in, with a review of everything you got wrong.

Homework & feedback

See what is due and when, hand your work in from the same page, and read the comments that come back on it.

Your progress

Every mark you have had and how far through each course you are — so you know where you stand before the next test, not after it.

On your phone

The whole thing works on a phone, in light or dark, and a test you were sitting when the connection dropped is not lost.
