FAQ

Teachers ask, we answer

Anything else? Book a call or message us on WhatsApp.

How many students can I have?

Plans are sized by the number of students. Most Lumen teachers run 300 to 500 on one platform, and you can move up a plan whenever your class grows.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We set up your platform with you, lay out your courses and stay on hand. If you can use WhatsApp, you can run Lumen.

Can students share my material?

Every test carries the student's own name and email as a watermark, copy attempts are reported to you, and files open through private links that expire.

Is the platform in my name or yours?

Yours. Your name, your logo and your colours appear on every page your students see.

Who marks the tests?

Lumen does, the moment a student hands in, and shows them why the right answer is right. Free-response answers are marked point by point against your scoring guide.

Can I run timed mock tests?

Yes. Give each paper the real exam's time limit and students sit it under exam conditions, then sit it again for a better mark.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in any browser, and there is an Android app with a notification for every new lesson, test and message.

How do I know who is falling behind?

The morning before each class, Lumen emails you that group's report: who did the homework, every mark and who hasn't started, with Excel and PDF attached.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many students you have. Book a call and we will go through your class and give you a price.
