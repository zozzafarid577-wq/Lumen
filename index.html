FAQ

Students ask, we answer

Something not working? Message us on WhatsApp, or tap the helper in the corner once you're signed in.

How do I get an account?

Through your teacher. They send your class a registration link: open it, fill it in once, choose your password and you're in. If you've lost the link, ask your teacher for it.

Can I sign up without a teacher?

No. Every course on Lumen belongs to the teacher who made it, so it's only open to their students.

I've forgotten my password.

Use Forgotten your password? on the sign-in page and we'll email you a link to choose a new one.

How do I know what to study?

Your scores show every test you've sat and what you got wrong, and each unit is marked strong, getting there or needs work. Start with the ones that need work.

Can I retake a mock exam?

Yes. Your best attempt is the one that counts towards your progress.

How are written answers marked?

Free-response answers are marked point by point against the official scoring guidelines, and you see why you got each point or missed it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any browser, and there's an Android app with a notification for every new lesson, test and message.

Who sees my marks?

You and your teacher. If your teacher has turned on reports to parents, your parents get a short summary by email too.
