What's included

Everything you need to run a class, in one place

Every Lumen space comes with all of this. The package you choose decides how much of it we run on your behalf.

Teacher view

The management system

The dashboard you sign in to. Assistants can be given part of it, never all of it.

Dashboard

Students, lessons, tests sat and work waiting to be marked — the four numbers that tell you where the week stands, with the latest activity under them.

Students

Create an account and hand over the sign-in details on the spot, by WhatsApp or on paper. Reset a forgotten password, pause access, keep parent contacts on file.

Courses & content

Courses hold units, units hold lessons, lessons hold recordings and PDFs. Mark a unit done and give it a release time, and it opens to the class by itself.

Question bank

Write questions once, tag them by topic and difficulty, and reuse them across tests. Paste a batch of multiple-choice questions and they are parsed for you.

Tests

Build a test from the bank, set a time limit and a pass mark, and schedule when it opens and closes. A scheduled test's questions are unreadable until it opens.

Assignments

Set work with a due date, collect what students hand in, mark it and write feedback they can read on their own page.

Progress & reports

Per-student progress across lessons and tests, averaged on each student's best attempt so one bad first try never defines them. Ready to send to a parent.

Announcements

Post to the whole space or to one course. Urgent notices sit at the top of every student's dashboard until they are taken down.

Assistants & access

Add an assistant and tick what they may do. Everything they do is recorded in your space's activity log, under their name.

Student view

What your students see

Their own dashboard, with only your content in it — and nothing from any other teacher.

Their courses, with progress on each
Lessons, recordings and materials, released on schedule
Tests they can sit, with a countdown and instant marking
Assignments to hand in, and the feedback that comes back
Every score they have had, with the review for each
Your announcements, at the top of their dashboard
A light or dark theme, and a phone that works properly
Their own password, changed on first sign-in
Kept separate

Your space is yours alone

Lumen hosts many teachers on one platform. Nothing crosses between them.

Separated at the database

Every course, lesson, test and student carries your space's id, and the database refuses any read that crosses it — not the page, the database.

Scheduling that holds

A lesson that has not been released and a test that has not opened cannot be fetched early, however clever the attempt.

Nothing is lost

A result that could not be saved — a dropped connection mid-test — is kept on the device and sent again on the next sign-in.
